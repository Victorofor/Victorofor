# Hi, I'm Victor Ofor

**UX/UI Designer & Researcher | Human-Centred Design | Digital Transformation**

I’m interested in how people interact with technology, how things work behind the scenes, and helping organisations translate complex needs and operational challenges into useful, accessible and sustainable digital solutions.

### Areas of Expertise

- **UX Research & Human-Centred Design:** User research, stakeholder engagement, contextual inquiry, personas, user journeys and usability testing.
- **Product & UX Strategy:** Understanding user and organisational needs, mapping workflows, identifying opportunities and translating findings into actionable product requirements.
- **UX/UI Design:** Information architecture, wireframing, prototyping, interface design, design systems and interaction patterns.
- **Digital Transformation:** Supporting organisations in improving processes and designing digital solutions adapted to their operational environments.

### How I Work

I believe effective digital solutions start with understanding the people, processes and environments they are designed to support.

My approach is collaborative and evidence-driven: understand the existing context, identify user needs and operational constraints, translate insights into design decisions, and work with implementation teams to deliver solutions that are practical and usable.
